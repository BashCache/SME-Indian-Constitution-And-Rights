📚 article 21

In which landmark case did the Supreme Court of India significantly expand the scope of Article 21, interpreting 'procedure established by law' to be synonymous with the American concept of 'due process of law'?
The case was Maneka Gandhi v. Union of India (1978). The Court held that the procedure established by law must be 'right, just and fair' and not arbitrary, fanciful, or oppressive. This effectively incorporated the principle of substantive due process into Article 21, making it a powerful check on both legislative and executive action.

Article 21's 'right to life' is not confined to mere animal existence. Name at least three significant unenumerated rights that the Supreme Court has interpreted as being part of Article 21, citing relevant cases.
The Supreme Court has read several rights into Article 21. Three significant examples are: 1. Right to Privacy (K.S. Puttaswamy v. Union of India), 2. Right to a Clean Environment (M.C. Mehta v. Union of India), and 3. Right to Livelihood (Olga Tellis v. Bombay Municipal Corporation). These rights are not explicitly mentioned but are considered integral to a life with human dignity.
